# Platform Humanization Guide

## What Makes Text Sound AI-Generated:

### 1. Overuse of Emojis
- AI: Uses emojis everywhere (👤🔒🎧💬✅🎉)
- Human: Uses icons sparingly, prefers text labels

### 2. Marketing Hype Words
- AI: "Revolutionary", "Cutting-edge", "Game-changing"
- Human: "Useful", "Helpful", "Works well"

### 3. Excessive Exclamation Marks
- AI: "Welcome to the platform!!!"
- Human: "Welcome"

### 4. Generic Phrases
- AI: "Start your journey today!"
- Human: "Start studying"

### 5. Overly Formal Language
- AI: "Utilize our comprehensive resources"
- Human: "Use our study materials"

## What Makes Text Sound Human:

### 1. Simple Words
- Use instead of Utilize
- Help instead of Empower
- Change instead of Transform

### 2. Direct Language
- "How can we help?" not "How may we assist you today?"

### 3. Calm Tone
- "Your changes are saved" not "✅ Changes updated successfully!!!"

### 4. Specific Details
- "16 CS courses" not "Comprehensive course library"

### 5. Natural Flow
- "We received your ticket" not "Your ticket has been successfully submitted"
